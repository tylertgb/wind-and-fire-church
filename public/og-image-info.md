# Open Graph Image

## Required Image: og-image.jpg

### Specifications:
- **Dimensions**: 1200 x 630 pixels
- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **File size**: Under 8MB (recommended under 1MB)
- **Aspect ratio**: 1.91:1

### Recommended Content:
- Church logo (Wind & Fire branding)
- Church name: "Wind and Fire Church A/G"
- Location: "Tamale, Ghana"
- Tagline: "Experience the Power of Worship & Revival"
- Background: Fire and wind imagery with your brand colors
  - Primary: Deep crimson-red (Fire)
  - Accent: Wind blue

### How to Create:
1. Use Canva, Figma, or Photoshop
2. Template size: 1200 x 630 px
3. Add your church logo and branding
4. Include key text (church name, location, tagline)
5. Save as `og-image.jpg` in the `/public` folder
