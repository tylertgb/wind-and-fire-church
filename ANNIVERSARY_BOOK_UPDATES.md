# Anniversary Book Content Updates

## Overview
Updated the Wind and Fire Church website to reflect the official Vision, Mission, Core Values, and Slogan from the church anniversary book.

## Changes Made

### 1. Vision Statement ✅
**Updated to:**
> To be a twenty first century apostolic model Church that will primarily seek to establish the rule of God in every heart especially within the three northern regions.

### 2. Mission Statement ✅
**Updated to:**
> To establish a centre of light, teaching and demonstrating God's wisdom and power, and raising an army of high-powered, un-ashamed, spiritual and intellectual ministers with one driving passion to depopulate hell and flood heaven with souls.

### 3. Core Values ✅
**Updated to match the five official values:**

1. **Truth** - We stand firmly on the unchanging Word of God as the foundation of all we believe and teach.

2. **Purity** - We pursue holiness and righteousness in our personal lives, ministries, and church culture.

3. **Excellence** - We honor God by giving our very best in worship, service, and everything we do for His kingdom.

4. **Relationships** - We prioritize genuine connections, community, and love — reflecting the heart of Christ to one another.

5. **Worship** - We pursue the genuine presence of God in Spirit-filled worship that glorifies Him in every gathering.

### 4. Church Slogan ✅
**Added the official slogan:**
> **Sanctuary of Wind & Fire**
> Movers of God, Removers of Mountains

## Design Consistency
- Maintained the purple brand color scheme matching the anniversary book
- Used existing UI components and styling patterns
- Integrated the slogan with Wind and Flame icons for visual appeal
- Updated the Core Values section from 4 to 5 values in a responsive grid layout

## Visual Location
All updates are visible in the **About Section** of the homepage:
- Vision & Mission statements appear in the top section
- Slogan appears below in a highlighted card with icons
- Core Values appear in the dedicated section further down
